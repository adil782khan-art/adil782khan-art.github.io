# Elevator Pitch — Feedback on Script 1 / Video 1

Feedback gathered from two reviewers after sharing
[the first pitch video](https://youtu.be/9bn1Ul8GwlQ).

## Reviewer 1

**Strengths**
- Hand movements are visible and used purposefully — good foundation to build on.
- Tying curiosity back into coding at the end works well — keep that connection.
- The reading mention almost lands as a differentiator.

**Weaknesses**
- Recorded in the Spark Labs meeting rooms — harsh lighting that reads poorly on
  camera ("feels like you're in jail," their words, and feedback they've given to
  other students too).
- Hand movements are present but small — not yet telling the story alongside the
  words.
- The pitch is solid but doesn't yet land a clear "this is what sets me apart"
  moment.

**Suggested improvements**
- Change recording location. Aim for warm lighting with nothing casting shadows
  across the face.
- Make hand movements bigger and more illustrative — use them to emphasize points,
  not just as background motion.
- Turn the reading mention into something specific and personal, e.g. "I have a
  passion for reading. Whether it's ___, ___, or ___, I have a love for
  understanding ___ and pursuing learning" — filled in with real, specific
  examples instead of staying generic.

## Reviewer 2

**Strengths**
- The curiosity / "I can explain what I build" framing is a good foundation for a
  personal brand.

**Weaknesses**
- Lighting needs improvement in the current recording space.
- Audio volume is very low — mic likely too far from the speaker.
- Delivery reads as low-energy / like reading from a script rather than confident
  and enthusiastic.
- Too abstract on the technical side — no specific languages, frameworks, or named
  projects, so it's hard for a listener to picture actual experience.
- No clear personal differentiator ("it factor") that separates this pitch from
  any other entry-level developer's.

**Suggested improvements**
- Record in a more controlled space — the podcast room was suggested specifically
  for better lighting and background.
- Position the mic closer, speak louder, and project more confidently.
- Add more energy: tone, facial expressions, posture, and pacing should all signal
  genuine excitement about the work, not recitation.
- Name specific languages/frameworks used, and reference 1–2 real projects with
  what they do and what problem they solve (e.g. "a full-stack food-delivery app,"
  "a dashboard," "an API") — concrete over abstract.
- Identify the specific "it factor": what do I do especially well, what
  experience shapes how I approach development, what value would I consistently
  bring to a team, and what should someone remember about me after the pitch ends?

**Overall**: solid rough draft. Main areas to fix for script 2 — recording
environment (location + lighting + audio), delivery energy, concrete project
details, and a clearer personal differentiator.

## Takeaways for Script 2

1. New recording location — not the Spark Labs meeting rooms. Podcast room or
   anywhere with warm, even, controlled lighting.
2. Mic closer, speak louder and with more energy — sound excited, not scripted.
3. Bigger, more illustrative hand movements.
4. Name actual tech (React, Node, MongoDB, GitHub Actions) and reference CodeBloggs
   and/or Rocket Elevators specifically, with what they do.
5. Land an actual differentiator — not just "I'm curious," but something concrete
   and specific to me.
